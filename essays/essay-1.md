Samwise Gamgee is the greatest friend of all time. Many times friendship is portrayed as a sharing of experiences or a matter of convinience, or maybe even a real connection of similar interests. Anyone can be one of those 3 things, but Samwise Gamgee is a friend who acts on constant, sustained love, no matter the cost. He displays something that is SO extremely rare in a friend: unconditional loyalty.

Yes, there are friends out there who do truly believe they are loyal, but oftentimes they see it (either conciously or subconciously) as transactional. They are loyal with the EXPECTATION that they will eventually be awarded or at least recognized, but not Sam.

Sam is a gardener, and yet he joined the fellowship of the rings, going on a journey to guide his friend to the most deadly place in middle earth, helping him bear the most evil item ever, a journey that would most likely cost him his life, and he did it anyways PURELY out of loyalty to his promise to stay by Frodo's side. I am not saying that friends need to be like this, because the truth is that nobody will actually realistically do this at the cost of risking their life, but I am saying that he is the definition of what a true friend is. 

He literally got betrayed by Frodo, getting accused of eating the lembas, but he STILL came back, and fought for Frodo's life, saving his life and the lives of everyone on middle earth, because if he didn't, then Shelob would've eaten Frodo, and the ring would've come back to Sauron. But that wasn't even the most clutch play he had. He literally physically CARRIED Frodo up mount doom, despite him literally hanging on to his breath by a string. Samwise is the greatest friend of all time, and it isn't even close.
